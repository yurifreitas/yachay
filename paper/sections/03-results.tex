\subsection{Two measured anomalies}
\label{sec:anomalies}

Reported here rather than in a footnote, because neither is fixed.

\paragraph{The control pool does not centre.} Nonessential controls calibrate to a mean $z$ of
\DepmapNonessentialMeanZ{} rather than approximately zero. Leading hypothesis: the null is
fitted by pooling control observations across genes, so it models the spread of a draw from
the pool rather than of a \emph{gene}.

\paragraph{The count correlation does not collapse.} On DepMap the Spearman correlation with
$\log \nobs$ moved \DepmapCountSpearmanRaw{} to \DepmapCountSpearmanCalibrated{}. Both are
near zero, and the range of $\nobs$ here is narrow; without an interval this is not yet a
result either way.\Pending{}
